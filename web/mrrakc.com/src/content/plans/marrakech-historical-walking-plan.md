Explore Marrakech through a compact historical walking route that connects the city's earliest monuments with its royal and ceremonial landmarks.

This one-day itinerary moves from the Ben Youssef Madrasa to the Almoravid Qubba, through the souks and Jemaa el-Fna, then down to the Koutoubia, Bab Agnaou, the Saadian Tombs, El Badi Palace, and Bahia Palace.

## Why this route works

- It keeps the walking manageable while still covering the city's essential historic layers.
- It follows a natural north-to-south arc through the Medina and Kasbah.
- It ends back at Jemaa el-Fna, which gives the day a strong closing moment.

## Suggested pace

- Start early at Ben Youssef Madrasa.
- Take your time in the souks and around Jemaa el-Fna.
- Reserve the late afternoon for the Kasbah and the royal palaces.
- Return to Jemaa el-Fna at sunset for the atmosphere and food.
- Most historical places close at a specific time, so be aware of their closing hours.
